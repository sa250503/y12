Get to know Pampanga

Pampanga is a province in the heart of Central Luzon , the Northern main island of
the Philippines archipelago.

The province is best known for culture, delicious food, and family-friendly
activities. However, this site aims to educate people about Kapampangan culture,
language, and the struggles of being a highly urbanised area.
