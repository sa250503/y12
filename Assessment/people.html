People of Pampanga!

Kampampangans are the people of Pampanga and are the sixth largest ethnolinguistic group in the Philippines. Their
culture is rich and full of grand traditions, and are known for having a strong sense of pride for Pampanga.

Sinukwan Festival

Held every December in San Fernando, the Sinukwan Festival is a vibrant, week‑long celebration named after the pre‑colonial deity Aring Sinukwan, the symbolic guardian of Kapampangan culture. The festival brings the city to life with colorful street dancing, lively musical performances, and creative art competitions that showcase the region’s rich traditions. Throughout the week, visitors can explore bustling food and craft fairs that highlight Kapampangan flavors, artistry, and craftsmanship. Every event is designed to honor the heart of the Kapampangan spirit; its pride, resilience, and deep cultural heritage. making the Sinukwan Festival one of Central Luzon’s most meaningful and joyful cultural experiences.
