Oh. the places you'll go!

There are many beautiful natural and man-made sights to be seen in Pampanga, below are just a few one should
visit to experience the sights, culture, and history of the province.

Holy Rosary Parish Church

Also known as The Minor Basilica and Parish of Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary, Santo Rosario Church or the
Pisamban Maragul, is a minor basilica and Roman Catholic church in Angeles City in the Philippines. The church
is a dedicated shrine to the Blessed Virgin Mary. The shrine is recognized by the National Commission for
Culture and the Arts as a national historical site and as an important cultural property by the National Museum
of the Philippines.

The church is a beautiful must-see historical building in Pampanga’s Angeles city, known for its commercial areas.
It is popular for pilgrimage and yearly Catholic traditions and customs. Come see the building for yourself in
between your shopping!

Pamintuan Mansion

The Pamintuan Mansion is a historic building in Angeles City, Pampanga built by the Pamintuan family in the
1880s. It was briefly used by the Katipunan during the Philippine-American War. It currently hosts a social
science museum.

Tutulari Avatar Gorge and mountain
Inararo Viewdeck

Tutulari Avatar Gorge is one of Pampanga’s newest and most breathtaking eco-tourism destinations, located
within the ancestral domain of the Aeta community in Porac. Known for its towering moss-covered canyon walls,
lush greenery, and surreal landscapes resembling scenes from the movie Avatar, the gorge offers a unique
combination of off-road adventure, nature trekking, and immersive outdoor exploration.
